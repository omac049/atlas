How these pages are made

Every comparison page is generated by software that reads each venue's published contract rules and prices, extracts the economic terms deterministically, and compares them field by field. No page is written by hand, no model guesses at meaning, and a pair is called the same bet only when the published terms match on every field and both venues' settlement terms are complete.

The system behind it is a paper-only research instrument. It has never placed a trade, and its research record — including four pre-registered hypotheses that each returned a negative result — is public. That record is the reason to trust a page that says "not verified": the instrument is built to say no.

What is and isn't here

  • Facts about published rules, fees, legal filings, and program terms — with sources and dates.
  • No picks, no probabilities, no strategies, no advice.
  • Affiliate relationships, disclosed on every page, that never affect a verdict.

Disclosure: this site may earn referral or affiliate revenue when you open an account through a link here. That never changes what a page says — every comparison is generated from the venues' own published rules and prices, and the method is public.

Nothing here is financial, legal, or tax advice, and nothing here is a recommendation to trade. These pages describe what two venues publish; they do not tell you what to do with it.
