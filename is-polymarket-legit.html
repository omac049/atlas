Is Polymarket legit? The documented record for both Polymarket venues

There are two Polymarkets: a US-regulated venue and the original global platform, which US accounts cannot use. Much of what is written about "Polymarket" mixes them up. This page keeps them apart and documents each — regulation, custody, disputes, enforcement — with a source on every line, so you can judge.

Questions people ask

Is Polymarket legit?

There are two Polymarkets. Polymarket US (QCX LLC) has been a CFTC-designated contract market since July 9, 2025 with a CFTC-registered clearinghouse and mandatory KYC. The international platform (Adventure One QSS Inc.) states it is not regulated by the CFTC, blocks US users, holds no customer funds (self-custody on Polygon), resolves disputes by UMA token vote, paid a $1.4M CFTC penalty in 2022, and faces 2026 class actions and a reported CFTC investigation over marketing practices.

Is Polymarket safe?

On the international platform funds never leave the user's own wallet, so safety depends on private-key handling; there is no deposit insurance or clearinghouse guarantee, and incidents in 2024-2025 (a login-provider vulnerability and comment-section phishing) drained some accounts. Market outcomes are decided by UMA token holders and cannot be reversed, which produced the disputed Ukraine-minerals, Zelensky-suit and Strategy-Bitcoin resolutions; Trustpilot shows 1.4/5 from 619 reviews.

Is Polymarket US the same as Polymarket?

No. Polymarket's own site says it 'operates globally through separate legal entities': Polymarket US is QCX LLC, a CFTC-regulated exchange launched to the public on May 12, 2026, using bank deposits, segregated customer accounts, KYC and an in-house outcome-review process; the international polymarket.com platform is run by Adventure One QSS Inc., uses on-chain USDC/pUSD, UMA oracle resolution, and is geoblocked for the United States.
